# Royal Indian Wedding Invitation Website ✨
Inspired by Artful Invites & Framer Luxury Editorial Aesthetics

A royal, interactive digital wedding invitation web application featuring animated multi-layer parallax cover, auspicious family blessings, swipeable event itineraries, live countdown clock, direct WhatsApp RSVP integration, Instagram hashtag sharing, and a personalized digital VIP keepsake guest pass.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Multi-Layer Royal Parallax Cover ("Touch for Magic")**:
   - Golden Jharokha arch with sunset sky, floating drifting clouds, temple silhouette, and couple cutout.
   - Sacred Ganesh invocation (`|| ॐ श्री गणेशाय नमः ||`).
   - Interactive glowing **"✨ TOUCH HERE FOR MAGIC"** button that plays an acoustic chime, triggers a golden petal burst, and starts classical Indian background music!

2. **Auspicious Family Invitation & Elder Blessings**:
   - Sanskrit Shlok & formal family invitation from grandparents and parents.
   - Ornate embossed gold borders on luxury parchment background.

3. **Interactive Wedding Events Itinerary (Slideshow)**:
   - Horizontal swipeable carousel with active slide dots:
     - **Mehendi Night** (15 Dec 2026 &bull; 7:00 PM)
     - **Haldi Ceremony** (16 Dec 2026 &bull; 11:00 AM)
     - **Sangeet & Cocktail Gala** (16 Dec 2026 &bull; 7:30 PM)
     - **Sacred Wedding Ceremony** (17 Dec 2026 &bull; 10:00 AM)
     - **The Grand Reception** (18 Dec 2026 &bull; 8:00 PM)
   - Integrated **"📍 View Location"** (Google Maps) and **"📅 Add to Calendar"** (.ics event generator).

4. **"Our Story" / Meet The Bride & Groom**:
   - Framed couple portrait with heartfelt romantic narrative and relationship milestones.

5. **"Counting The Days" — Real-time Live Countdown Clock**:
   - 4 gold-framed clocks dynamically ticking down days, hours, minutes, and seconds to the wedding day.

6. **"Will You Join Us?" — Direct WhatsApp RSVP & Digital Form**:
   - Prominent **"💬 RSVP ON WHATSAPP (TAP HERE)"** button that pre-fills a ready-to-send confirmation message.
   - In-app interactive RSVP form with confetti animation and personalized confirmation.

7. **Instagram Tag & Official Hashtag Card**:
   - `#Rahulkikiran` / `#HemantKiJasleen` with 1-click **"Copy Hashtag"** and **"📸 Open Instagram"** links.

8. **👑 Personalized VIP Keepsake Guest Pass**:
   - Enter your name -> Generates a custom royal gold VIP pass ready to save or print.

9. **Web Audio API Indian Instrumental Synthesizer**:
   - Zero external audio dependencies! Plays *Raag Yaman (Sitar)*, *Royal Flute & Shehnai*, and *Sacred Vivah Harmonies* natively in any browser.

10. **Interactive Canvas Particle Engine**:
    - Floating rose petals and gold stardust embers with touch sparkle bursts and on/off toggle.

## 🚀 Live GitHub Pages Deployment

To enable GitHub Pages for this repository:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** in your GitHub repository: `https://github.com/HemantPrajapat25/wedding/settings/pages`
2. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Your website will be live at:
   👉 **`https://hemantprajapat25.github.io/wedding/`**
