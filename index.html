☬ The Sacred Ceremony

Anand Karaj

"The Blissful Spiritual Union"

In the Sikh tradition, marriage is not merely a social agreement, but a spiritual pilgrimage where two independent souls fuse into a single spiritual entity (Ek Jot Doi Moorti). During the ceremony, the bride and groom take four circumambulations (Laavan) around the sacred Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, each representing a deeper stage of spiritual evolution and commitment.

First Laav

Faith, Righteousness & Daily Duty

ਹਰਿ ਪਹਿਲੜੀ ਲਾਵ ਪਰਵਿਰਤੀ ਕਰਮ ਦ੍ਰਿੜਾਇਆ ਬਲਿ ਰਾਮ ਜੀਉ ॥

"In the first round of the marriage ceremony, the Lord sets out His instructions for performing the daily duties of married life. Embrace righteousness, renounce sins, and meditate on the Lord's Name."

Gurudwara Etiquette for Guests

Head Coverings Scarves/Rumāls are respectfully worn inside and will be provided at the foyer.

Guru Ka Langar A traditional sacred vegetarian meal will be lovingly served after the hymns.
